Convert Garmin FIT files without uploading them

Garmin devices and fitness platforms commonly use the FIT format for activity data. FIT2CSV reads the selected FIT file locally in your browser and creates a CSV from the decoded FIT messages and fields exposed by the parser.

What can be included in the CSV?

Depending on what is stored in the original Garmin FIT file, decoded data can include session and lap information, timestamped records, GPS coordinates, heart rate, cadence, power, altitude, device information, events, and other FIT fields.

One CSV for analysis

FIT2CSV places the decoded FIT message types into one CSV table. Structural message_type and message_index columns identify each FIT message while parser field names and decoded values are retained without adding coaching scores or inferred workout metrics.

Private browser-based conversion

The FIT file is read with browser APIs and processed locally. FIT2CSV does not send the activity file to a backend conversion service or store it in a database.

Frequently asked questions

Does FIT2CSV work with Garmin FIT files?

Yes. FIT2CSV is designed to decode standard FIT activity files, including FIT files produced by Garmin devices and platforms.

Is my Garmin activity uploaded?

No. The selected FIT file is decoded locally in your browser.

Does the CSV add calculated training metrics?

No. The downloaded CSV is a decoded export and does not add coaching scores, heart-rate drift, or inferred workout types.

Can I use the CSV with data or AI tools?

Yes. CSV is easier for many spreadsheet, data-analysis, and AI workflows to inspect than the original binary FIT format.
